# Supplementary source audit

This supplement leaves the frozen proof and original audit unchanged. Checked on 22 October 2026.

## Related Chasse journal article

Matthew Chasse, *Laguerre multiplier sequences and sector properties of entire functions*, Complex Variables and Elliptic Equations 58 (2013), 875–885, DOI 10.1080/17476933.2011.584250, published online 7 July 2011:

https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/17476933.2011.584250?needAccess=true

The official abstract concerns rationally interpolated multiplier sequences and sector preservers. Indexed excerpts of the author manuscript state Theorem 1.7: a multiplier sequence interpolated by a reduced rational function must be polynomially interpolated. This statement does not apply to the radical-power interpolant in AIM Problem 34. However, the complete journal article was not inspected. The old author manuscript URL, https://people.kth.se/~chasse/LaguerreSector.pdf, returned HTTP 404 in a direct request and could not be fully retrieved with the web tool. Therefore the article is not exhaustively excluded as prior art on the basis of its abstract or excerpts.

## Existing Carlson/interpolation techniques

Andrew Bakan, Thomas Craven and George Csordas, *Interpolation and the Laguerre–Pólya Class*, accessible author manuscript:

https://math.hawaii.edu/~tom/mathfiles/interp.pdf

The web tool returned the parsed text of the nine-page manuscript, which was inspected. A separate attempt to save a local PDF returned HTTP 503; therefore no local copy or visual page check is claimed. Theorem 3.2 supplies a Laguerre–Pólya interpolant for a positive **CZDS** with positive limiting nth root. Theorem 3.4 states Carlson's half-plane uniqueness theorem directly. Theorem 3.6 combines interpolation and uniqueness to constrain an already-entire interpolating function under a growth hypothesis. Thus Carlson-based interpolation arguments in this subject are established techniques and must not be advertised as new here.

These statements assume the stronger CZDS property, whereas AIM Problem 34 asks only for the multiplier-sequence property. They do not by themselves give the present multiplier-sequence conclusion. Our proof constructs its entire Newton interpolant directly from the multiplier assumption and then compares it with a non-entire slit-domain function. The paper does not state the radical-power shifted-sequence target. This distinction is limited to the displayed results; it is not a global priority guarantee.

## Continued limitations

Neither source changes the mathematical statement or exact certificate. The bounded-novelty caveat in the original audit remains necessary. In particular, no claim that the elementary interpolation obstruction as a general method is historically new is made.
